Single Cat gives authors a compact dropdown when a content model needs exactly one category. Keep the relationship native to Craft without presenting a full element selector for a small, deliberate choice.

Choose the category group for the field and present its categories in a dropdown. Authors get a quick, familiar control with no ambiguity about selecting several values.

## Features

- **Category relationship:** Store the selected value as a native Craft category.
- **Compact dropdown:** Choose from the configured group without opening an element modal.
- **One clear value:** Match content models that require exactly one category choice.
- **Group selection:** Limit the options to the taxonomy that belongs in the field.
- **Element in Twig:** Use the chosen category’s fields and URL in normal templates.
- **GraphQL:** Query the selected category as a native element in headless builds.
- **Familiar field setup:** Add the field to Craft layouts alongside the rest of the content model.
- **Native category data:** The selected value remains a Craft category element, ready for its title, URL, custom fields, and normal element-query behaviour in templates.
